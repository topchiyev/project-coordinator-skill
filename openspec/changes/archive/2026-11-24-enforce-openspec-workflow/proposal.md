## Why

Substantial work needs a tracked change before implementation, and the dashboard needs to show progress as OpenSpec is updated.

## What Changes

- Require an OpenSpec change for medium and large work unless the user explicitly opts out.
- Refresh the dashboard after each OpenSpec update and have the designated Coordinator serve it when feasible.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `project-coordination` defines when to open a change and when to refresh and serve the dashboard.

## Impact

Project Coordinator skill instructions, optional role profiles, and project coordination requirements.
